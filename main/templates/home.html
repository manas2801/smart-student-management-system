Smart Student Management System

A full premium Django-based college project to manage students, attendance, marks, fees, timetable, assignments, notices, and downloadable reports from one unified platform.



Get Started Login

Why This Project?

This project is designed to digitize student management in colleges and institutes. Instead of maintaining records manually, the system provides a centralized web-based platform where users can manage student details, attendance, marks, assignments, fees, notices, timetable, and reports efficiently.

Main Features

Student Records

Add, edit, search, and manage complete student profiles with photos.

Attendance Tracking

Record present and absent status with a clean academic workflow.

Marks Management

Store subject-wise performance data for each student clearly.

Fee Management

Track total, paid, and due fee records student-wise.

Timetable Module

Manage classes using day, time, subject, and room details.

Reports & Analytics

Use dashboard charts and export student reports in CSV format.
